### Top 3 Visual Fixes per Direction

#### Direction A ([A-morning.png](file:///workspace/world-listens/shots/A-morning.png) / [A-sunset.png](file:///workspace/world-listens/shots/A-sunset.png))
1. **Waterfall & Island Integration**: Replace the opaque 2D ribbon waterfall with a translucent particle/foam shader, and add ambient occlusion contact shadows beneath floating islands.
2. **Ground Shading**: Break up uniform plastic green hills with subtle procedural grass wind ripples and soft tonal gradients.
3. **City Depth**: Introduce aerial perspective scattering gradients and subtle window specular glints to turn the flat silhouette into a breathing metropolis.

#### Direction B ([B-morning.png](file:///workspace/world-listens/shots/B-morning.png) / [B-sunset.png](file:///workspace/world-listens/shots/B-sunset.png))
1. **Road Harmonization**: Replace the modern asphalt highway and crisp lane stripes with a storybook cobblestone or organic dirt path.
2. **NPR Shading**: Apply brush-textured ramps and soft toon shaders to foliage and terrain to match the hand-painted sky.
3. **Island Strata**: Replace flat brown rock cones with textured watercolor stratification.

#### Direction C ([C-morning.png](file:///workspace/world-listens/shots/C-morning.png) / [C-sunset.png](file:///workspace/world-listens/shots/C-sunset.png))
1. **Camera Pitch**: Lower the camera pitch to restore horizon presence and expand visible sky volume.
2. **Tilt-Shift Blur**: Emphasize true macro tilt-shift with pronounced foreground/background circular bokeh.
3. **Facet Lighting**: Soften harsh face normal contrast with warmer ambient bounce into dark shadow facets.

### Glass UI Critiques

1. **Title**: The light serif glyphs lose contrast against high-key morning clouds; add a subtle luminance drop-shadow or dynamic contrast tint.
2. **Style Switch**: The segmented pill border is overly rigid; on [A-morning-mobile.png](file:///workspace/world-listens/shots/A-morning-mobile.png) it creates an intrusive banner competing with the skyline.
3. **Time Dial**: Positioned directly across the road's perspective vanishing point, the frosted container obstructs central scene depth; remove the card backing and let the arc float cleanly.
